## Overview

For my Software Engineering class, we decided to try and develop a note sharing platform, aptly named "Hoos Taking Notes" since our school calls students "Hoos". Here, we had to learn to use Django, PostgreSQL, Amazon S3, and deployment on Heroku, in order to create the application. During this class, we learned about several software development methods, and we also employed the "Scrum" ideology. For my group, I was designated as Scrum Lead, and I make sure we're having weekly check-ins, taks designations, and that we're meeting deadlines as a group. At the time of writing this, the project is still underway, but most of the meat of it is done, and I'm excited to update this page with more info!
